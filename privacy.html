Robinso Replies: Privacy Policy

Robinso Replies is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Robinso Beats to reply to comments on that channel.

What it accesses. Through the YouTube Data API it reads public comments on the Robinso Beats channel and the titles of the channel's own videos, and posts replies to those comments on behalf of the channel.

What it stores. Only the IDs of comments it has already replied to, kept locally on the owner's computer so it does not reply twice. No personal data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Google user data. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
